📊 Price Breakdown — Where Does Your Price Go?

💡 Pro Tip: Most successful e-commerce sellers keep 30-50% profit margin after all fees. Amazon charges 10-15%, Flipkart 10-20%. GST rate depends on your product category (commonly 5%, 12%, 18%, or 28% in India). RTO (Return to Origin) is very common in COD orders (10-25% typical) — always price it in, otherwise a "profitable" product can silently lose money. Note: "Profit per Unit (if delivered)" includes the Return/RTO Reserve — that portion isn't really yours to spend, it's the money this order carries to cover the returns/RTOs that happen on other orders. "Blended Profit per Order (Real)" is your true long-run average profit after that's accounted for.
